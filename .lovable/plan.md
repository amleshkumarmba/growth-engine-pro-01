# BscaleX Personal-Brand Lead Generation Page

## Goal
Build a polished, single-page consultant website for BscaleX/Bharat that moves visitors from authority and problem awareness to booking a free strategy call or requesting a free growth audit.

## What I’ll build
- A sticky desktop navigation, mobile menu, and persistent mobile booking button.
- A dark, high-contrast opening section using the supplied BscaleX brand mark, an editable expert portrait area, focused positioning, and two consistent actions.
- Trust signals, pain points, four growth pillars, nine services, a four-step process, placeholder case studies, an editorial personal profile, differentiators, placeholder testimonials, FAQs, contact details, and a strong closing action.
- Two accessible lead forms: a detailed growth audit and a concise contact inquiry.
- Validated form states with loading, error, and the requested “You’re In!” success confirmation, plus an optional scheduling action.
- Lightweight event hooks for CTA clicks, form starts/submissions, calendar, phone, WhatsApp, and scroll depth.
- Responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly menus/accordions/forms, subtle reveal motion with reduced-motion support, optimized images, and semantic page structure.

## Content and trust safeguards
- Use only public, verifiable identity details found in the supplied references.
- Keep unverifiable metrics, case-study outcomes, testimonials, client marks, contact details, and experience visibly labeled as placeholders in a centralized content configuration.
- Do not fabricate clients, certifications, results, tenure, or revenue claims.

## Visual direction
- Premium black/charcoal and white composition with BscaleX cyan as the single accent.
- Bold editorial typography, restrained gradients, fine borders, minimal shadows, and generous spacing.
- Preserve the reference’s authority-led positioning without copying its wording or layout.
- Use the uploaded BscaleX mark in the page and as the favicon; generate a cohesive professional portrait only if the public references do not provide a suitable one.

## Lead capture and integrations
- Enable Lovable Cloud and store submitted leads securely with server-side validation and access controls.
- Keep booking, CRM, Google Sheets, email, WhatsApp, Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and Google Ads identifiers in a clearly documented configuration layer—never hardcoded secrets.
- Make the default form submission functional now; external services remain safely inactive until real account details or connections are supplied.

## SEO and validation
- Add route-specific title, description, Open Graph tags, canonical URL, one H1, and appropriate Person/ProfessionalService structured data using verified fields only.
- Validate the finished page on desktop and mobile, test navigation, forms, accordions, success states, accessibility basics, and confirm the preview builds without errors.
